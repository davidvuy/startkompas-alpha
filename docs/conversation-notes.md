# Conversation notes

Immutable, unreviewed conversation records keep manually supplied quotations, contributor interpretations and follow-up questions separate. Use participant codes P01–P9999, not required real identities. Codes are labels, not proof of distinct people, consent or participation. No source text is generated and no statement is automatically promoted to evidence.

Input includes title, nullable conversation_date and source_id, participant_codes, context, limitations, entered_by and statements. Each statement names a listed participant_code and has nullable quotation, interpretation and follow_up fields; at least one must be supplied. Missing quotations remain missing. Text preserves whitespace and is inert.

An optional inbox source is a context reference, not verified extraction. Its artifact fingerprint and raw-text fingerprint are retained and checked. A quotation can differ from that source and remains explicitly contributor-supplied. No reference is fetched or opened. Unknown dates stay unknown; capture time is separate.

Limits: 300 notes per project; 30 unique codes per note; 100 blocks; 8000 UTF-8 bytes per block field; 200000 UTF-8 bytes for serialized input. Strict fields, real calendar dates, well-formed readable Unicode, immutable file/event hashes, current revision and archive guards apply. Preview/save require authenticated local session, matching origin, exact project/state and explicit confirmation. Identical preview retries return their original receipt. Saved notes are included in whole-project backups, not automatically in offer exports. The browser provides Conversation notes → Add a conversation note, with multiple removable statement blocks, optional inbox context selection, exact preview, explicit confirmation and read-back. Codes are derived from the entered blocks without counting repeated blocks as additional participant codes. Edits invalidate preview and confirmation. Unsaved notes are guarded during navigation but are not included in recovery copies. Archived projects remain read-only.
