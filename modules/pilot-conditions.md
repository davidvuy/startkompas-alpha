# Pilot conditions — research draft 0.1.0

Checked 2026-10-27. Supported preparation: Belgian solo founder, Belgian B2B digital-service pilot, no employees. This is a question and evidence worksheet, not approved terms, tax advice or a compliance assessment. Qualified interpretation, applicability and coverage review are absent. Narrow supported execution to synthetic/local preparation while sensitive conclusions remain open. Do not present an operational recommendation as law.

## Use only the affected section

Reuse existing facts and artifacts. Record the actual region, entity, activity, customer type, dates, data, providers and proposed promises with sources; unknowns remain unknown. Consumer sales, employees, regulated activities, other jurisdictions or new data flows require additional research. For each condition save: fact IDs/versions, proposed action, source URL and checked date, category (reported requirement / recommendation / proposed agreement), applicable scope, open question, reviewer needed and change trigger. Use the source-register helper for claims; four review dimensions remain separate. A checked link is not a reviewed project.

### Administration and billing

Find the founder's registration, activity and VAT situation before choosing administrative actions. [FPS Economy procedures](https://economie.fgov.be/en/themes/enterprises/starting-business/procedures-self-employed) is a starting point, not confirmation that this founder has registered. Ask the appropriate business counter/accountant to assess unresolved entity, regional and fiscal details before real paid delivery. Do not assign fees, VAT rates or exemptions from a generic template.

Source conflict: the [general start portal](https://business.belgium.be/32) still states a Walloon basic-management requirement. The [Walloon announcement](https://www.wallonie.be/fr/actualites/suppression-des-competences-de-gestion-pour-devenir-independant), updated 2025-10-01, reports abolition from that date, with specific professional qualifications separate. Do not turn the generic portal into a task to obtain the abolished proof. Preserve the discrepancy; the actual activity and current applicable rules still need scoped confirmation.

The [official e-invoicing FAQ](https://efactuur.belgium.be/nl/FAQ/algemene-vragen-b2b) describes a structured B2B invoicing obligation from 2026-01-01 with scope and exceptions. Small-business VAT exemption alone does not remove it. Establish the actual parties, establishment, VAT status and transaction; confirm applicability and a suitable invoice route with an accountant before invoicing. Do not label a PDF-only workflow sufficient by default or choose a paid vendor without a requirement and authority. Dates and exceptions must be rechecked before use.

### Agreements and acceptance

Prepare a discussion sheet covering parties, specific outcome, inputs, exclusions, delivery window, acceptance demonstration, price/payment, duration, exit, handling of failures, support and data/rights responsibilities. These are proposed agreement topics, not a legally sufficient contract. The [FPS Economy B2B terms guidance](https://economie.fgov.be/sites/default/files/Files/Publications/files/gedaan-met-de-wet-van-de-sterkste-deel-4.pdf) explains unfair-terms controls; B2B does not mean unrestricted contracting. Leave enforceability and coverage open for a suitable Belgian legal reviewer. No blanket liability waiver, unilateral acceptance, automatic renewal or invented service guarantee. Preserve reviewer disagreements and the exact clauses they concern.

### Ownership and permitted use

Inventory original work, customer inputs, collaborator contributions, third-party components, images/data and generated outputs separately. Record provenance, actual licences, notices and intended use/distribution. [FPS Economy on software ownership](https://economie.fgov.be/nl/themas/intellectuele-eigendom/intellectuele-eigendomsrechten/auteursrecht-en-naburige/auteursrechten/intellectuele-eigendom-op/creatie-van-een/wie-bezit-de-auteursrechten-op) distinguishes ownership, employment and commissioned software: a commission is not an automatic transfer of all rights. Do not infer ownership or clearance merely from payment, AI generation or a repository's top-level licence. Resolve rights for the actual deliverable with appropriate review; otherwise omit the uncleared material.

### Data, AI and suppliers

Map each actual flow: data categories and people, purpose, sender, receiver, host account/provider, storage/location, access, retention, deletion/export and onward providers. Distinguish business confidentiality from personal data. Synthetic examples do not establish a real-data basis. The [EDPB role guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-controller-or-data-processor_en) ties controller/processor roles to actual purposes and instructions and describes processor contracts and subprocessor authorization. Do not assign roles from a product name or declare an unsigned template sufficient.

Collect actual provider terms/version, permitted use, data-processing terms where relevant, subprocessors, transfer arrangements, training/use settings, retention and exit/export. Unknown provider terms block a conclusion, not independent synthetic work. Customer approval alone is not proof of a lawful basis or adequate safeguards. Before real personal/confidential data enters an AI or supplier tool, obtain a scoped data-protection assessment and actual authority. No blanket claim that local files mean no host/cloud data transfer. AI-specific applicability is not covered by this draft; keep it open rather than claiming an AI Act assessment.

### Security, recovery and support

The [EDPB security guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/secure-personal-data_en) calls for measures proportionate to the actual risk and context. Proposed controls for review: least privilege, strong authentication where available, current software, protected storage and transfer, separation of customers, minimum retained data, incident contacts, backups and restore rehearsal. This is an original preparation list, not a copied standard, certification or exhaustive statutory checklist. Do not blindly reuse a numeric password threshold from general guidance.

For each promised recovery outcome record data set, backup owner/location, frequency, acceptable loss/downtime proposed by the founder, tested restore result and unresolved failures. Never invent recovery objectives or claim that a successful local helper test proves recovery of the business service. Demonstrate restore with synthetic data first. Set a support channel, actual owner, available hours, triage/stop route, known limitations and handover/exit process; no invented 24/7 coverage. Separate response targets from commitments already accepted by the parties.

## Output and review boundary

Produce a concise conditions matrix with all eight topics: administration, agreements, ownership, data use, suppliers, security, recovery and support. Reference existing artifacts instead of repeating them. A missing topic is not not-applicable. Link each unresolved condition to the pilot activity it blocks and allow independent preparation. Changed region/entity/customer/data/provider/terms/price/service promise invalidates only dependent conclusions, retaining old evidence. Before real launch, seek named, scoped, dated review and proof of actual controls. No overall clearance is provided by this draft.
