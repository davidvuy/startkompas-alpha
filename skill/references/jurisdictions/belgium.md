# Belgium — jurisdiction pack

Scope: a solo founder (natural person or single-member company) established in Belgium, selling a digital service to Belgian business customers, no employees, small bounded pilot.

Out of scope and requiring separate research: consumer (B2C) sales, employees or subcontractors, regulated professions, cross-border supply, physical goods, payment or financial services, health data.

**This pack is preparation, not advice.** It records what official sources state, with dates. It is not a tax, legal or compliance determination for any specific founder. Consequential steps need a Belgian accountant, business counter (`ondernemingsloket` / `guichet d'entreprises`) or lawyer.

Verification convention below: **[checked 2026-11-10]** means the source page was actually fetched and read on that date in this project. **[carried 2026-10-27, not refetched]** means the claim comes from earlier project research and its page was *not* re-read since — treat it as needing a recheck before consequential use.

## 1. Starting as self-employed — registration and formalities

Entry point: [FPS Economy — procedures for the self-employed](https://economie.fgov.be/en/themes/enterprises/starting-business/procedures-self-employed) · *secondary-official overview* · **[carried 2026-10-27, not refetched]**

What it is: a starting point listing the formalities. It is **not** confirmation that any particular founder has registered, nor a complete personalized list.

Establish before advising any administrative action:
- Whether the founder is already registered in the KBO/BCE and under which activity codes
- Their VAT situation: identified, under the small-business exemption regime, or not identified
- Their social-insurance fund affiliation status
- Region of establishment (Flanders / Wallonia / Brussels) — some competence rules differ

Do not assign fees, VAT rates, exemptions or deadlines from a generic template. Route unresolved entity, regional and fiscal questions to a business counter or accountant.

### Recorded source conflict — basic management skills

This conflict is preserved deliberately. Do not resolve it silently.

- [business.belgium.be general start portal](https://business.belgium.be/32) still describes a Walloon basic-management (`basiskennis bedrijfsbeheer` / `compétences de gestion de base`) requirement · **[carried 2026-10-27, not refetched]**
- [Wallonia — abolition announcement](https://www.wallonie.be/fr/actualites/suppression-des-competences-de-gestion-pour-devenir-independant) states the requirement was **abolished with effect from 2025-10-01**, describing the rule as outdated. Specific professional competences for certain regulated trades are **maintained** — the announcement names restaurateurs, bakers and pastry chefs, roofers and heating engineers. The announcement frames this as aligning Wallonia with Flanders and Brussels, which had abolished comparable requirements earlier. · *official regional announcement* · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

Consequence for the founder: do not create a task to obtain the abolished proof. Still confirm whether the founder's **actual activity** is a regulated trade with its own retained competence requirement.

Recheck trigger: a change of region, or the founder's activity turning out to be a regulated trade.

## 2. VAT — small-business exemption regime

Current threshold: **€25,000** annual Belgian turnover, excluding VAT, for 2026.

Scheduled phased increase, from **2027-01-01**, rising €1,000 per year:

| Year | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 | €25,000 |
| 2027 | €26,000 |
| 2028 | €27,000 |
| 2029 | €28,000 |
| 2030 | €29,000 |
| 2031 and after | €30,000 |

Legal basis: a bill approved by the Chamber of Representatives on **2026-09-17**.

Sources · *both secondary (professional commentary), mutually consistent* · **[checked 2026-11-10]**
- [vereniginginfo.be — threshold from €25,000 to €30,000](https://www.vereniginginfo.be/blog/vzws/btw-vrijstellingsregeling-kleine-ondernemingen-van-25000-naar-30000-euro/302/)
- [btw-lijn.be — bill raising the exemption turnover threshold](https://www.btw-lijn.be/wetsontwerp-verhoging-omzetdrempel-vrijstelling-kleine-onderneming-ingediend/)

**Open: not primary-confirmed.** Both sources are professional commentary, not the published law or an FPS Finance page. Before a founder relies on a specific year's figure, confirm against FPS Finance or the Belgian Official Gazette. Headlines reading "threshold raised to €30,000" are misleading — €30,000 applies only from 2031.

What the exemption does: an eligible business supplies without charging VAT and is relieved of periodic VAT returns, while keeping its VAT identification. The trade-off is **loss of input VAT deduction** on purchases and investments relating to the exempt activity. · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

Separate EU cross-border scheme: a dual-threshold system with an EU-wide figure of €100,000 reportedly applies from 2025-01-01, alongside the national threshold. · *single secondary source* · **[checked 2026-11-10, needs primary confirmation]** — [vat-consult.be](https://www.vat-consult.be/blog/small-enterprises)

Recheck triggers: turnover approaching the threshold; the start of any new calendar year; any cross-border customer.

## 3. B2B structured electronic invoicing — in force now

**This obligation is already in effect.** It applies to invoices *created, issued or sent* from **2026-01-01** (`opgemaakt, uitgereikt of verzonden vanaf 1 januari 2026`).

Source: [official e-invoicing FAQ — general B2B questions](https://efactuur.belgium.be/nl/FAQ/algemene-vragen-b2b) · *primary official* · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

Scope, as stated by the FAQ:

| Situation | Position |
| --- | --- |
| Belgian VAT-taxable business invoicing another Belgian VAT-taxable business (B2B) | **In scope — must issue structured e-invoices** |
| Business using the small-business VAT exemption regime (turnover ≤ €25,000) | **In scope — the obligation applies** |
| Special agricultural flat-rate regime | In scope **at least for receiving** B2B invoices (`minstens voor het ontvangen van B2B-facturen`) |
| VAT taxpayer not established in Belgium, without a fixed establishment, even if VAT-identified here | **Out of scope** — the FAQ notes the law will be adjusted on this point |
| Selling only to consumers (B2C) | Not mandatory |
| A person with only article 44 VAT-exempt activities | **Unknown** — the FAQ does not address this case. Do not infer either way. |

Format and transport: a structured electronic invoice sent over the **Peppol** network. Legal hook: new article 53, §2bis of the Belgian VAT Code.

**Why this matters most for this skill's default user.** A Belgian solo founder running a small B2B pilot is very likely under the €25,000 exemption regime — and being under that regime does **not** remove this obligation. A PDF emailed to a Belgian business customer is not a structured electronic invoice. Do not treat a PDF-only workflow as sufficient by default.

Equally: do not push the founder toward a paid invoicing vendor without an established requirement and the founder's own authority to spend. Establish the actual parties, place of establishment, VAT status and transaction type, then confirm the applicable route with an accountant.

Recheck triggers: the announced legal adjustment for non-established taxpayers; any change in the founder's VAT status; a non-Belgian or non-business customer; before the founder issues their first real invoice.

## 4. B2B agreements and unfair terms

Source: [FPS Economy — B2B unfair terms guidance (PDF, part 4)](https://economie.fgov.be/sites/default/files/Files/Publications/files/gedaan-met-de-wet-van-de-sterkste-deel-4.pdf) · *official guidance* · **[carried 2026-10-27, not refetched]**

Key point: **B2B does not mean unrestricted contracting.** Belgian law applies unfair-terms controls between businesses.

Prepare a discussion sheet, not a contract: parties, the specific outcome promised, required inputs, exclusions, delivery window, how acceptance is demonstrated, price and payment, duration, exit, what happens on failure, support, and responsibility for data and rights.

Do not draft a blanket liability waiver, unilateral acceptance clause, automatic renewal, or any invented service guarantee. Leave enforceability and coverage open for a Belgian legal reviewer, and preserve any reviewer disagreement together with the exact clauses it concerns.

## 5. Ownership and permitted use

Source: [FPS Economy — who owns the copyright on software](https://economie.fgov.be/nl/themas/intellectuele-eigendom/intellectuele-eigendomsrechten/auteursrecht-en-naburige/auteursrechten/intellectuele-eigendom-op/creatie-van-een/wie-bezit-de-auteursrechten-op) · *official guidance* · **[carried 2026-10-27, not refetched]**

Key point: the page distinguishes ownership, employment and commissioned software — **a commission is not an automatic transfer of all rights.**

Inventory separately: original work, customer inputs, collaborator contributions, third-party components, images and data, and generated output. For each record provenance, the actual licence, required notices, and intended use and distribution.

Never infer ownership or clearance from payment alone, from the fact that AI generated something, or from a repository's top-level licence. Where rights for the actual deliverable are unresolved, either resolve them with appropriate review or omit the uncleared material.

## 6. Data protection — roles, suppliers and security

Roles: [EDPB — data controller or data processor](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-controller-or-data-processor_en) · *EU regulator guidance* · **[carried 2026-10-27, not refetched]**

Roles follow the **actual purposes and instructions**, not a product name or a contract title. The guide covers processor contracts and subprocessor authorization. Do not assign a role from a vendor's label, and do not declare an unsigned template sufficient.

Map each real data flow: data categories and the people involved, purpose, sender, receiver, host account and provider, storage and location, access, retention, deletion and export, and onward providers. Keep business confidentiality distinct from personal data. Synthetic examples never establish a real-data basis.

For each provider collect: terms and their version, permitted use, data-processing terms where relevant, subprocessors, transfer arrangements, training and data-use settings, retention, and exit/export. Unknown provider terms block a conclusion — they do not block independent synthetic preparation. A customer's approval is not by itself a lawful basis or an adequate safeguard. Before real personal or confidential data enters an AI or supplier tool, get a scoped data-protection assessment and actual authority.

Security: [EDPB — secure personal data](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/secure-personal-data_en) · *EU regulator guidance* · **[carried 2026-10-27, not refetched]**

Measures must be proportionate to the actual risk and context. Controls worth proposing for review: least privilege, strong authentication where available, current software, protected storage and transfer, separation of customers, minimum retained data, named incident contacts, backups, and a rehearsed restore. This is a preparation list — not a standard, a certification, or an exhaustive statutory checklist. Do not copy a numeric password threshold out of general guidance as if it were a requirement.

**EU AI Act applicability is not covered by this pack.** Keep it open rather than claiming an assessment.
