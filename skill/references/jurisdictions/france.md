# France — jurisdiction pack

Scope: a solo founder established in France, selling a digital service to French business customers, no employees, small bounded pilot.

Out of scope and requiring separate research: consumer (B2C) sales, employees or subcontractors, regulated professions, cross-border supply, physical goods, payment or financial services, health data. `Travaux immobiliers` (building works) are specifically carved out below and need their own check.

**This pack is preparation, not advice.** Consequential steps need a French `expert-comptable` or the tax administration itself.

Verification convention: **[checked 2026-11-10]** means the source was fetched and read on that date. **[carried 2026-10-27, not refetched]** means it comes from earlier project research. **[not researched]** means no source was consulted — the topic is open.

## 1. Registration and formalities

All business creation, modification, document filing and cessation declarations go through the **single mandatory portal**:

[formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr — guichet unique](https://formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr/) · *official portal, operated by INPI* · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

The portal describes itself as *"Le guichet unique pour les déclarations de création, de modification, de dépôt de document et de cessation d'entreprise"*. Informational content is open; submitting a declaration requires an INPI account.

Establish before proposing any administrative action: the founder's legal form (`micro-entreprise`, `entreprise individuelle`, `société`); their SIREN/SIRET; their APE activity code; their VAT position; and whether the activity is regulated.

Open: the specific documents, fees and timelines for each form are **[not researched]** in this pack.

## 2. VAT — franchise en base de TVA

Thresholds currently applicable, differentiated by activity:

| Activity | Base threshold | Seuil majoré (tolerance) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Prestations de services` and `activités libérales` | **€37,500** | €41,250 |
| `Ventes` and `hébergement` | **€85,000** | €93,500 |

Source: [service-public.gouv.fr — franchise en base de TVA](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F21746) · *primary official (government information service)*, page's own update date **2026-01-01** · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

A digital B2B service normally falls under `prestations de services`, so the **€37,500** figure is the relevant one for this pack's default founder.

**Mandatory invoice mention.** Every invoice or statement issued under the franchise must carry:

> `TVA non applicable - article 293 B du CGI`

Omitting it exposes the business to penalties on audit. This is a concrete, checkable requirement — verify the founder's invoice template actually contains it. · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

**Mixed activities:** where a founder both sells and provides services, total turnover must stay under €85,000 **and** the services part under €37,500. Breaching either limit takes the whole activity out of the regime. · *secondary commentary* · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

Effect: no VAT charged, and no input VAT recovered.

### Recorded history — the abandoned €25,000 single threshold

Preserved because founders and advisers still cite it:

- The `loi de finances` for 2025 introduced a **single €25,000 threshold** for all activities.
- It was **suspended on 2025-02-28**.
- It was **definitively removed by the law of 2025-11-03**.
- For 2026 the differentiated thresholds above therefore remain unchanged.

· *secondary commentary, consistent across sources; corroborated by the official page noting the reform was abandoned* · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

### Open — PLF 2026 proposal

The `projet de loi de finances` for 2026 is reported to propose a €37,500 base threshold **with €25,000 for `prestations de services de travaux immobiliers`** (building works services).

**This is a bill, not law.** Do not plan around it, and do not tell a founder in building works that a €25,000 threshold applies to them. Confirm the enacted text before relying on it. · *secondary commentary* · **[checked 2026-11-10, status open]**

Recheck triggers: turnover approaching €37,500; the start of a new calendar year; enactment of the PLF 2026; any building-works component in the activity.

## 3. B2B e-invoicing — receiving already mandatory, issuing for small businesses from 2027-09-01

Source: [European Commission — eInvoicing in France](https://ec.europa.eu/digital-building-blocks/sites/spaces/DIGITAL/pages/467108885/eInvoicing+in+France) · *official EU source* · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

| Date | Obligation |
| --- | --- |
| **2026-09-01** | **All** businesses must be able to **receive** e-invoices |
| **2026-09-01** | `Grandes entreprises` and `ETI` (large and medium-sized) must **issue** |
| **2027-09-01** | `PME`, `TPE`, `micro-entreprises` and `indépendants` must **issue** |

**Both September 2026 obligations are already in force** — they took effect roughly two months before this pack's check date. A French solo founder must already be able to receive a structured e-invoice today, and has until **2027-09-01** to be able to issue one.

That issuing deadline is under a year away at the time of writing. For a founder planning a pilot, it belongs on the timeline now, not later.

**Platform model.** In **October 2024** France abandoned building a public invoicing portal for company-to-company invoicing. Businesses must instead use one of the **accredited private platforms** (`Plateforme de Dématérialisation Partenaire`), of which the EC page reports nearly 100, or a compatible solution connected to one. The public portal is retained only for data-concentrator and central-directory services. · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

This is a real difference from Belgium and Germany: France requires routing through an **accredited platform**, so an email address is not sufficient and some tooling choice is genuinely unavoidable. Establish the requirement and the founder's own spending authority before recommending any specific platform, and never recommend one as endorsed.

Formats in French use: **UBL 2.1**, **UN/CEFACT CII**, and **Factur-X** (the Franco-German hybrid standard). `Chorus Pro` remains the B2G system. · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

**B2C is not in scope** — the EC page states there are no current B2C e-invoicing mandates. · **[checked 2026-11-10]**

Open: the official French tax administration guidance on PDP selection and the e-reporting obligation that accompanies e-invoicing are **[not researched]**. The `economie.gouv.fr` page on the reform returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-10 and was not read.

Recheck triggers: before 2027-09-01; any change in the founder's size category; publication of updated administration guidance.

## 6. Data protection — roles, suppliers and security

The GDPR applies directly. The supervisory authority is the **CNIL**, which publishes its own extensive SME guidance.

- Roles: [EDPB — data controller or data processor](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-controller-or-data-processor_en) · *EU regulator guidance* · **[carried 2026-10-27, not refetched]**
- Security: [EDPB — secure personal data](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/secure-personal-data_en) · *EU regulator guidance* · **[carried 2026-10-27, not refetched]**

Roles follow the actual purposes and instructions, not a product name or a contract title. Measures must be proportionate to the actual risk and context.

CNIL's own guidance is **[not researched]** and would be the better source for a French founder. EU AI Act applicability is not covered.
