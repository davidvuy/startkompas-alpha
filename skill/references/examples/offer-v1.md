# Offer hypothesis — draft v1

Monthly report clean-up service · `clearer-reports` · revision 3 · 2026-11-06

**Draft.** Not evidence of demand, payment, an interview conducted, or pilot approval. Two of the assumptions it rests on are still unknown.

## For whom

Small agencies, roughly 5–15 people, that send a monthly report to their own clients and currently fix the tool's export by hand.

*Basis:* `fact-p01-manual-rework` — reported, one person, 2026-11-04.
*Open:* that this describes more than one agency is `assumption-agencies-pay-for-this`, still unknown.

## The job

Turning an unreadable tool export into something a client can read, every month, by hand.

*Basis:* `fact-p01-manual-rework` — reported.

## Proposed outcome

One cleaned-up monthly report, delivered as a document the agency can send to its client unchanged.

*Label:* hypothesis. No one has accepted or paid for this.

## Delivery and scope

**Included:** one report, one month, from a source file the agency supplies. Up to two rounds of correction.

**Excluded:** connecting to their reporting tool, building a template, recurring delivery, anything touching their clients directly.

*Basis:* `decision-narrow-to-one-deliverable` — because the only record held is one recurring manual task. A subscription would rest on assumptions nothing supports.

## Price

€150 for the first report, as a proposed figure.

*Label:* proposal. Derived from `assumption-one-hour-per-report` (unknown) plus a margin the founder chose. **A cost floor does not prove willingness to pay.**
*Open:* VAT treatment depends on the founder's actual VAT status — see `jurisdictions/belgium.md` §2. Not resolved here.

## Current alternative

Doing it by hand, which is what P01 does now, at an effort they described but did not quantify.

*Open:* no other provider or tool was researched. Absence of a named competitor here is **not** a finding that none exists.

## Supporting and contradicting indications

**Supporting:** one person described the problem unprompted and in concrete terms about a specific recent month.

**Contradicting:** nobody has been asked to pay. P01 did not say the task was expensive or urgent — only that they do it.

## Next check

Offer the paid one-off to three agencies and record who accepts, declines, or asks the price first. Task `test-willingness-to-pay`.

---

*Why this example exists:* every section names its basis and its label, and the unknowns are visible in the document rather than tidied away. Produce output shaped like this — not a feature list, and not a confident plan with the uncertainty removed. An offer whose price has no stated basis, or whose "supporting indications" are reworded assumptions, is the failure mode to avoid.
