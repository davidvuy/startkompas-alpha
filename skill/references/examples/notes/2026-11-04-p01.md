# P01 — conversation note

**Fictional.** This note exists so that the worked example's `sources` entry resolves to a
real file, the way a founder's own project would. P01 is not a person.

- **When:** 2026-11-04, 35 minutes, video call.
- **Who:** operations lead at a 9-person agency. Not named here; the dossier refers to them
  as P01 and that is the only identifier kept.
- **Asked about:** the most recent concrete instance of producing their monthly client
  report — what it was, how they did it, how long it took, what they used instead.

## What they said, in their words as recorded

- They export three files from their reporting tool and rebuild the layout by hand in a
  document each month.
- Asked how long: *"an hour, maybe a bit more if the numbers look wrong and I have to go
  back and check."*
- Asked what happens if it is late: *"nothing formal. It just looks sloppy and the client
  asks."*
- They do it themselves. Asked who would decide to pay for help: *"I'd have to ask."*

## What this note is not

Not a market finding, not a price, not a commitment, and not evidence that anyone else has
this problem. One person, one call, their own account of their own work. The dossier records
it as `reported` for exactly that reason.
