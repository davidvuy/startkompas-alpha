# Sources and scoped review

## Record a claim with its context

Every consequential claim gets a `sources` entry per [dossier-format.md](dossier-format.md), with:

- the applicable context — who and what it applies to
- the source URL or location, and its type
- the source's own publication and effective dates **where the source states them** — otherwise `null`
- your check date — the date you actually read it
- a concrete recheck trigger

Unknown dates stay `null`. Never estimate a date, and never use today's date as a publication date.

Prefer the relevant primary source. A user report, a vendor page or a professional commentary is not automatically primary evidence — label it for what it is. Where only secondary sources are available, say so and mark primary confirmation as open.

## Keep four review dimensions separate

These are four different judgments. Collapsing them is the most common way a register becomes misleading.

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Source quality** | Is this source authoritative for this claim? |
| **Interpretation** | Does it actually say what we read into it? |
| **Applicability** | Does it apply to *this* founder, activity and date? |
| **Coverage** | Does it answer the whole question, or only part? |

A source can be impeccable and still inapplicable. A correct interpretation of the wrong source answers nothing.

## Preserve disagreement

Record conflicting sources without silently choosing a winner. State what each says, with its date, and what would resolve the conflict.

A preserved conflict is more useful than a confident wrong answer. See the basic-management entry in [jurisdictions/belgium.md](jurisdictions/belgium.md) for the pattern: two official sources, one superseded in practice, both recorded, with the practical consequence spelled out.

## Reviewed means a named person

A `reviewed` entry requires a reviewer, a date and a scope. Those fields record **attribution**, not verified expertise — they do not make the reviewer qualified, and a reviewer alias is not an authenticated identity.

Never invent a reviewer to satisfy a validation field. Never label your own check independent. An unreviewed entry is honest; a fabricated reviewer is not.

A valid register never upgrades a business control and never proves legal clearance.

## Copying and rights

Prefer an original concise summary with a link. Copying an excerpt requires a recorded licence basis; unresolved rights stay visible rather than being assumed.

Possession of a document is not permission to republish it.

## On resume

Retain historic records. Check whether a source's recheck trigger has occurred, and reread the relevant source before consequential reuse.

Old evidence is historical, not current approval. A claim rechecked and unchanged on a new date is itself worth recording — note the new check date.
