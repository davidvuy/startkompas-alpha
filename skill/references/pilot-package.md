# Connected pilot package

Use this after customer-learning and the scoped conditions work have identified a candidate pilot. Reuse the existing brief, evidence and conditions — never regenerate them just to fill a package.

This produces an internally **connected draft**, not a launch approval.

## 1. Fix the identity

Identify one project, milestone, client scope, period, version and the known facts. Link the source artifacts. Keep case facts, proposals and unknowns distinguishable.

Keep three money figures distinct and never merge them: the **price** to the customer, the **experiment budget** available, and the **delivery cost**.

## 2. Assemble the index

Link, rather than restate:

- the pilot brief
- the relevant conditions matrix entries
- same-period costs with their explicit exclusions
- how product acceptance will be demonstrated
- the recruitment copy — **unsent**
- the support and exit plan

An unknown cost is not zero. Recruitment copy must match the actual maturity of the draft and the evidence behind it. Never claim proven savings, compliance, existing customers, service levels or limited availability without evidence.

## 3. Connect the checks

Connect each check to its source facts and current artifacts. At minimum review: customer outcome, price against cost against budget, data and provider conditions, rights, acceptance, and support.

Explain each dependency. Record unresolved review scope and any conflicting views.

Use the existing records and links per [dossier-format.md](dossier-format.md). Never create a second competing canonical state. An ordinary document remains an allowed simpler route when a full dossier is not warranted.

## 4. Handle change

For a changed provider, data scope, price, customer or commitment: walk the declared links to identify affected costs, terms, acceptance or support. Mark those `review_required` and report them before changing anything.

Preserve the unaffected learning and every historical version. Rechecking an artifact hash proves bytes only. A technical consistency check satisfies no legal, commercial or security review.

## 5. Save and close

Save the package, read it back, and report its real path and version.

Give the founder the next feasible step and **every** urgent blocker. Keep unsent copy unsent unless the founder has given explicit outreach authority and the host actually has that capability.

No contract signature, invoice, upload, publication or purchase follows from preparation.

## Reviewer handoff

When handing the package to a reviewer, state: the exact package revision and its source facts; the professional competence being requested; which sections are in and out of scope; the source, interpretation, applicability and coverage decisions **separately**; conflicts and dissent; the changes required; and the date with its evidence artifact.

A blank review template is not review performed.
