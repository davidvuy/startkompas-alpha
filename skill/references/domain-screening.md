# Breadth screening

Use this at intake, at a new milestone, or on a relevant change — to notice a topic the founder has not considered. It is a prompt for judgment, never a legal applicability conclusion.

A simple edit does not need a screening. Previously skipped intake must not restart automatically.

## The fifteen domains

The domains are jurisdiction-neutral. The **answers** for the rule-bearing ones (D06–D11, D13) are local — use the relevant [jurisdiction pack](jurisdictions/README.md) or its research protocol.

| # | Domain | Covers | Becomes relevant when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D01 | Founder and starting position | Goal, time, budget, experience, location, people | Any intake, or founder constraints change |
| D02 | Customers and market | Problem, target customers, alternatives, research findings | A segment, problem or market decision is proposed |
| D03 | Offer and business model | Value, pricing assumption, costs, sales approach, willingness to pay | An offer, price, sales model or supplier cost changes |
| D04 | Product and user experience | Scope, user flow, accessibility, language, acceptance | A product or user-facing flow is proposed or changed |
| D05 | Development and technical quality | Reuse, architecture, tests, integrations, maintenance | Software is built, integrated or materially changed |
| D06 | Formation and administration | Business context, registration, authority, formalities | Operating context, entity or responsible people change |
| D07 | Finance and taxes | Budget, cash flow, accounting, invoicing | Money, billing, business location or transaction scope is involved |
| D08 | Ownership and collaboration | Code and content, name, licences, founder agreements, departure | Assets or collaborators are introduced, reused or transferred |
| D09 | Contracts and legal matters | Customers, suppliers, terms | A customer or supplier arrangement is proposed or changed |
| D10 | Privacy and AI | Data, purposes, roles, suppliers, retention, AI outputs | Data or AI outputs are collected, processed, shared or changed |
| D11 | Security and continuity | Access, secrets, abuse, backups, recovery, incidents | Systems, access, dependencies or important data are involved |
| D12 | Marketing and sales | Positioning, outreach, channels, claims, measurement | Promotion, outreach or a sales channel is proposed or changed |
| D13 | Payments and customer support | Purchase, renewal, cancellation, complaints, support | A customer lifecycle or payment flow is proposed or changed |
| D14 | Team and daily operations | Roles, handover, freelancers, staff, suppliers | People, work allocation or operating suppliers change |
| D15 | Growth and closure | New markets, new activities, transfer, controlled closure | Expansion, transfer or controlled closure is considered |

Deeper procedures exist for some of these: D02 and D03 → [offer-and-market.md](offer-and-market.md); D06–D11 and D13 → [pilot-conditions.md](pilot-conditions.md) plus the jurisdiction pack.

## How to screen honestly

Consult the table, pick the domains the present situation actually touches, and load only those.

Record **only** the questions you actually considered against available context, each with an attributable answer or an explicit unknown.

Do not fill every domain with null answers to make a scan look complete. An unvisited domain stays **not screened** — which is different from "screened and found not applicable". A missing deeper procedure is never a negative applicability finding.

Do not turn fifteen domains into thirty questions for a founder who asked one thing.

## What a recorded screening is

An attributed statement of what was considered at one project revision. Not a qualified assessment, not verified compliance, not a completed task.

Read existing screens on resume. A changed revision or a damaged record needs scoped reconsideration. Preserve prior screening records rather than overwriting them.
