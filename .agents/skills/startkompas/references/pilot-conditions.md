# Scoped pilot conditions

For administration, agreements, ownership, data/provider use, security, recovery or support, read only the relevant sections of `modules/pilot-conditions.md` in the installed application root. It is a dated research draft with sensitive conclusions open, not approved guidance.

Reuse the founder's known facts and existing documents. Save a conditions matrix with source/date, fact dependencies, recommendation versus reported obligation, unresolved applicability and review needed. Keep independent synthetic preparation executable. Do not infer that a small B2B pilot, no employees or local storage removes obligations. See source-register.md for recording independent review dimensions and workflow-route.md for changed facts. A module read or a schema-valid register does not constitute expert review.
