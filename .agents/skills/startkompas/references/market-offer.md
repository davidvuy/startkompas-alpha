# Business deliverables

For the selected market/offer/feasibility decision, read modules/market-offer.md in the installed application (also available through domains detail D02 or D03). It contains six focused procedures with named outputs. Produce the useful artifact now, reuse scoped prior work and keep unperformed research obvious. Do not turn a small unrelated request into all six procedures.

Preserve original input constraints and unknowns. A price hypothesis, arithmetic break-even, interview count, model forecast or working prototype is not payment/demand evidence. Use the cost helper only on explicit same-period assumptions; its output is an estimate with exclusions, not accounting or permission to spend. No outreach is implied by preparing a guide.

The installed `src/business-cli.mjs` now prepares and resumes versioned interview/alternatives/offer/pilot/pricing drafts. Use the **Versioned business briefs** section of modules/market-offer.md for commands and field structure. Read existing scoped drafts before revising. Save exact content through core attach, then read back; render gives readable Markdown. A revision names the predecessor and reason, preserves the earlier document and records field changes. Missing fields stay unknown; no interview, payment or approval is inferred.
