# Sources and scoped review

Use docs/source-register.md from the installed application for schema and commands. Record a claim with its applicable context, source URL/type, actual known publication/effective dates, check date and a concrete recheck trigger. Unknown dates stay null. Prefer the relevant primary source; a user report or secondary explanation is not automatically primary evidence.

Separate source-quality review from interpretation, applicability to this project and coverage of the question. Record disagreements without silently choosing a winner. A reviewed entry needs reviewer/date/scope and a saved evidence artifact; those fields record attribution, not authenticated expertise. Do not invent a reviewer to satisfy validation.

Use original concise summaries with links when copying rights are unknown. Copying an excerpt requires a recorded licence reference; unresolved rights remain visible. A valid register never upgrades a business control or proves legal clearance. On resume, retain historic records and check revision changes; reread relevant sources before consequential reuse when their recorded trigger applies.
